The Psychology of Play

I grew up in a big Nintendo family, racing in Mario Kart and falling in love with interactive media in my living room. But it was really The Legend of Zelda franchise, from the classic 8-bit game to its sprawling open worlds that truly captivated me, showing me how environmental design can create a deep sense of enchantment.

This video is an early exploration of that fascination, created for my Media Art and Design Practice class. Using Womp for 3D modeling and Unity for world-building, I experimented with lighting, atmosphere, and camera angles to capture a blend of nature and magic heavily inspired by Zelda's Korok Forest.

Untitled Top-Down RPG

I am currently in the early ideation and prototyping phase of an original game concept. Drawing structural inspiration from the classic top-down, 8-bit style of the original Zelda, I want to capture that nostalgic, exploratory feel while still making it feel fresh and new.

I am building an original world with a completely revamped, custom color palette and a new cast of characters. Right now, my focus is on defining the core mechanics and drafting the narrative architecture. Pictured is a work-in-progress concept art for a common enemy.

Tech Stack: Unity, C#, Womp
